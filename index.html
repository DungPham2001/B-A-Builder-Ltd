About Us

Property development, done properly.

B & A Builders Ltd is a property development company specialising in the full lifecycle of real estate projects. Our work covers land assembly, feasibility and design planning, investment structuring, and the construction of residential and commercial buildings — delivered either for sale or for long-term rental.

We manage every stage ourselves, which means tighter quality control, clearer timelines, and developments that are built to last.

  • Land sourcing and acquisition
  • Design & planning permission strategy
  • Investment and project funding
  • Construction delivery and handover
